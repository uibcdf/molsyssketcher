# Roadmap

The [living plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) owns task states. MolSysSketcher has
two primary capabilities with equal product importance: Molecular View and
Interaction View. Staged delivery reflects dependencies and finite profiles.

Molecular representation provides reusable depiction primitives for diagrams.
The interaction track starts with shared model/reference work and does not
wait for full molecular exploration or production editing. Both primary
surfaces can be used independently; their common foundation is implemented once.

## First consumer target

Sabueso 0.16.0 is the first exploration consumer target, not MolSysSketcher's
version. Coordinate exact finite candidate scope under Sabueso #154. Both
minimum journeys belong to the product target; changes need an explicit scope
decision rather than silently removing Interaction View to meet a date.

A successful molecular-only intermediate milestone is named as such. It does
not complete the joint baseline. Record source/native profile, original context,
actual provider and linked-host evidence.

A standalone 2D view can work without MolSysViewer. The linked profile needs
a real correspondence-backed journey; unavailable/partial mapping behavior
remains necessary failure-path evidence, not successful synchronization.

## Minimum versus full capability

Initial minimums are bounded and reviewed in P0.6. The interaction minimum
includes a ligand-centered complex and a residue-level/two-sided interface.
Temporal/comparison breadth, domain layers and advanced chemistry have
explicit tasks and acceptance rather than a generic future-extension label.

M5 can deliver a declared finite exploration artifact while richer I3/I4
work stays pending. Do not describe that artifact as completing the full
interaction roadmap. Production editing remains the final major capability.

## Future scope

Potential later directions include richer editing tools, reactions/Markush
coverage, notebooks, many-molecule dashboards, alternative measured renderers
and additional scientific profiles.

ADMET and allostery remain future owner-defined profiles. Their absence does
not make the interaction pillar optional, and their names do not establish
adopted dependencies or supported scientific methods.

## Exact completion boundaries

The [delivery-gate table](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#delivery-gates-and-dependencies)
is authoritative for standalone molecular G-M, standalone diagram G-I, joint
linked G-J, remaining exploration G-R and editor G-E. A milestone uses its
listed subset; it does not imply completion of every task in an encompassing
stage. The final review leaves task count and product scope unchanged.
